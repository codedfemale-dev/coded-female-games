About the game & supporting guidance

These are original fictional learning exercises. The stories, examples, choices, code challenges and illustrations were created for Coded Female. The guidance links support the underlying lessons. No competitor text, screenshots, puzzles or artwork are included.

No accounts, live AI, analytics, cookies or answer storage. Progress lasts only while this page stays open and resets on reload. Downloads are made on your device. Fonts are served with the games. GitHub Pages hosts the site and handles ordinary page requests.

These exercises introduce practical ideas. They are not certifications, full accessibility audits or predictions of real people’s behaviour.
