Field guide / privacy & access

This collection brings together 15 Coded Female games with fictional learning examples. No competitor scenarios, screenshots or artwork were copied.

No sign-up or live AI service is needed. Bug Bash saves your best score and Release Manager saves your career in this browser using local storage. Release Manager also saves game files for offline play. Sprint Survival saves your journey and debriefs in this browser too. Other game progress resets on reload. Fonts are served with the collection. GitHub Pages handles ordinary hosting requests.

Each game has its own format and supporting guidance. Bug Bash and Release Manager include timed arcade play and an untimed mode. These exercises are introductions, not certifications or full professional assessments.

Neon-grid and pixel-world styles are original Coded Female artwork. The six restyled learning games have no timer, and support keyboard controls. Other games explain their own controls and pace.
