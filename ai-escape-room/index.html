About the lab & how it works

AI Escape Room is a Coded Female learning game. All characters, documents, organisations and AI responses are fictional. The puzzles and feedback are written in advance. No live AI model is connected.

No timer, lives or penalties. Try again, use a hint and learn your way out. Fragments change with each new game. This game does not use cookies, analytics or browser storage. Nothing you enter is sent to an AI service. Progress resets on reload.

Prompt injection means an attempt to redirect an AI through instructions hidden in material it reads. This game illustrates one example; real defences also require technical controls. Further reading: Anthropic on prompt injection and Microsoft Research on checking AI recommendations.
