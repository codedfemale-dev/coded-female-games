About the game & trusted guidance

Created by Coded Female to make cyber security more approachable. Phishing means using deceptive messages to trick someone into revealing information or taking an unsafe action. These scenarios are fictional and written for Coded Female. Example addresses are deliberately non-working text. Each round mixes concerning requests, situations supported by independent checks and cases where more evidence is needed.

No answers or personal details are stored or sent anywhere. Progress resets when you reload. Fonts load from Google Fonts; the game works with fallback fonts too.

Further reading: NCSC: spotting scams, NCSC: reporting suspicious emails, and CISA: multi-factor authentication. For work accounts, follow your organisation’s reporting and security procedures.

More guidance behind the new scenarios: FTC advice on job scams, CV conversion scams, task scams, voice impersonation and tech support scams. These sources inform the safety lessons; the messages, choices and explanations are newly written fictional examples. New content reviewed on 12 October 2026.
