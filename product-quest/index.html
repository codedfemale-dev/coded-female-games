EPISODE 01
THE SPOON INITIATIVE
Field guide, controls & privacy

Each stakeholder has three decisions. Read the evidence and choose a next step. Two approaches in every situation are defensible and earn 30 alignment each. Three such choices reach 90 against a target of 80. All three decisions are covered before the meeting ends.

A choice that leaves an important assumption open earns 10 alignment. After the meeting, revisit each open question with the evidence visible. A sound revision replaces 10 with 30 and can earn full completion credit. You keep the rest of your progress.

Energy is a pacing cue, never a lock on choices or a scoring factor. A breather restores 24 energy once per meeting; moving to the next meeting restores 18. No coffee, speed or confidence threshold is required.

Completion XP is 100 per grounded decision plus 150 per stakeholder agreement. Different sound approaches and successful reviews earn the same credit. Agreement on a pilot plan does not prove readiness for a real release.

Use Tab and Enter, or keys 1–3 to choose, C for a breather and Enter to continue. No timer. Sound is optional. Restart asks for confirmation during a run. Progress resets on reload; save the final decision record to keep it.
