A CORPORATE ADVENTURE · EST. 1995

PRODUCT QUEST™

One spoon. Seven stakeholders. Absolutely no alignment.

EPISODE 01
THE SPOON INITIATIVE
Field guide, controls & privacy

Reach 80 alignment to win each stakeholder. Tailor your pitch to their priorities. Strong answers use 14 energy; partial answers use 8; corporate nonsense uses 4 but costs 24 confidence. You have at most three turns per boss. A confidence meter of zero ends the run.

Two coffees restore 32 energy and 8 confidence each, with one break allowed per stakeholder. A win restores 18 energy and 8 confidence before the next battle. Clear all seven to approve the pilot.

Use Tab and Enter, or keys 1–3 for dialogue, C for coffee and Enter to advance. There is no timer. Sound is optional. Restart asks for confirmation during a run. Progress resets on reload; copy your final review to keep it.

Original fictional satire and pixel artwork for Coded Female. No accounts, analytics, external assets or saved personal data. Scores are game mechanics, not predictions of real colleagues. GitHub Pages handles normal hosting requests.
